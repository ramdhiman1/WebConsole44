Is Ram Technical Help completely free?
Absolutely! All our troubleshooting guides, AI Testing banks, and interactive Practice Labs are 100% free to access. We aim to democratize technical knowledge.
What is the "AI Testing" section?
Our specialized AI Testing section was built to help QA engineers transition into AI-driven testing. It covers topics like ChatGPT for automation, prompt engineering for testers, and testing machine learning models.
How does the "Practice Lab" work?
The Practice Lab is an interactive environment where you can test your troubleshooting skills on simulated scenarios. It provides instant feedback and helps you internalize the steps required to fix common PC and network issues.
Can I suggest a new guide or question?
We love community input! If you have a troubleshooting solution or an interview question we missed, use the Contact Us form or the submission portal in the Admin section.
Why do you use placeholders for ads?
Ram Technical Help is currently in its final pre-launch phase. We have placed AdSense placeholders to ensure our professional layout accommodates monetization without degrading the user experience later.
Do I need to sign up to use the Mock Interviewer?
No sign-up is required to use our tools. However, creating a free account (coming soon) will allow you to save your practice results and track your learning progress over time.
Is my data secure in the Lab?
Security is a priority. All interactive data in our labs is stored in your LocalStorage. This means your data never leaves your computer and stays completely private.
